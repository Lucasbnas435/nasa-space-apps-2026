Default: rural Andradas, Minas Gerais, Brazil. In Google Maps, right-click your field to copy the coordinate. Altitude corrects temperature: NASA climate data is the average of a large area, and higher land is colder. On every run, the tool reads the altitude of the point from the terrain measured by NASA's SRTM mission. The slope comes from the same terrain and is used for erosion risk. If you know the exact altitude or slope, type it: the value holds until you change the coordinate.
